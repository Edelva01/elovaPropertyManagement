About Elova

Quiet excellence for the places people live, host, and work.

Elova Property Management exists to remove the friction from maintenance — for owners who care about their assets, and for managers who need a partner that simply shows up and delivers.

Our standard

Premium isn’t louder. It’s cleaner, clearer, and more reliable.

Inspired by the best hospitality-led property brands, we built Elova around a simple idea: maintenance should feel as polished as the properties we serve. That means thoughtful estimates, respectful technicians, and communication that never leaves you guessing.

We take on small repairs and large projects with the same care — because a leaking faucet and a full unit refresh both decide whether a guest leaves a five-star review or a tenant renews.

Questions

Answers before you book.

Do you handle both small repairs and large projects?

Yes. Elova is built for the full spectrum — from a single fixture replacement to multi-room renovations and coordinated multi-trade work. Every job starts with a clear estimate.

Can you support Airbnb and short-term rental turnovers?

Absolutely. We prioritize guest-impacting issues and can run punch lists between stays so your listing stays bookable and review-ready.

Do property managers get special reporting?

Yes. We provide owner-friendly notes, photo documentation, and clear invoicing so you can keep stakeholders informed without extra admin.

How quickly can I get an estimate?

Most requests receive a response within 24–48 hours. Urgent guest or business issues are flagged for priority triage whenever capacity allows.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Richmond and surrounding communities. Multi-property portfolios in the region are welcome — tell us your locations when you request an estimate.
